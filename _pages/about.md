# Elisa Alonso-Herrero

I am a first year PhD student in Economics at the [Paris School of Economics](https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/en/). I am mainly interested in the intersection between public and labor economics, with an emphasis in social safety net programs and payroll taxation.

Previously, I was a pre-doc at the University of Zurich working on education, gender, and health economics in the teams of [Ulf Zölitz](https://ulfzoelitz.com/) and [Ana Costa-Ramón](https://sites.google.com/view/anamariacostaramon). I received my Master in Applied Economics at PSE in 2026.

You can view my [CV](https://elisaalonsoh.github.io/files/CV_Elisa_Alonso-Herrero_2026.pdf) here. If you would like to reach me, you can email [elisa.alonsoh@gmail.com](mailto:elisa.alonsoh@gmail.com)
